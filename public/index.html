Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

WorkPay collects information necessary to provide attendance and payroll services:

  • Location Data: We require precise location access (GPS) while using the app strictly to verify presence within the office radius during clock-in and clock-out. Location data is not tracked continuously in the background.
  • Account Credentials: Name, work email, phone number, and encrypted passwords.
  • Device Identifiers & Advertising IDs: Used in compliance with Google Play Developer policies and Google AdMob for personalized or non-personalized advertisements.

2. How We Use Information

We use your data solely to calculate employee attendance, record approved leaves, compute monthly salary transactions, and maintain account security.

3. Data Protection

All network traffic is encrypted over TLS (HTTPS). Sensitive tokens and keys are stored in secure hardware-backed storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain).

4. Contact Us

For inquiries, support, or data deletion requests, contact the WorkPay developer team at support@workpay.com or via your administrator.
